🎯 PRIORITIZE YOUR TOP THREE TASKS — Identify the three most important tasks each morning and complete them before tackling less critical work.

⏰ USE TIME BLOCKING — Schedule dedicated periods for specific tasks to maintain focus and avoid distractions.

🔥 BATCH SIMILAR TASKS — Group emails, meetings, and administrative work into dedicated sessions to reduce context switching.

🍅 TRY THE POMODORO TECHNIQUE — Work in focused 25-minute intervals followed by short breaks to maintain energy and concentration.

📵 ELIMINATE DISTRACTIONS — Silence unnecessary notifications and create a clutter-free workspace to improve focus and efficiency.
