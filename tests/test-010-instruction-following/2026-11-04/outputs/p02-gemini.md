A car engine is like a strong metal tummy that eats gas to make the car go.

Inside the engine, there are metal cups with heavy blocks that slide up and down. First, a tiny bit of gas and air goes into a cup. Next, the block pushes up to squish them tight. Then, a tiny hot spark sets the gas on fire!

That makes a safe little pop that pushes the block back down super fast. This up-and-down push spins a metal bar, which turns the big wheels and makes your car zoom down the road!
