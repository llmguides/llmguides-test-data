⏳ **USE TIME BLOCKING** — Schedule every hour of your workday in advance to dedicate uninterrupted focus to specific priorities.

🐸 **EAT THE FROG FIRST** — Tackle your most challenging and important task at the very start of the day before your willpower depletes.

📵 **ELIMINATE DIGITAL DISTRACTIONS** — Put your phone in another room and block non-essential notifications during deep work sessions.

⏱️ **APPLY THE TWO-MINUTE RULE** — Complete any incoming task immediately if it takes less than two minutes rather than adding it to your to-do list.

🍅 **WORK IN POMODORO SPRINTS** — Alternate between twenty-five minutes of intense concentration and five-minute breaks to prevent mental fatigue.
