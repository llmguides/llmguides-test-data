A car has a hungry heart under the hood. You fill the car with gas, which is its special food. Inside, the gas mixes with air in little metal cups. A tiny spark lights it, and it goes POP! Each pop pushes a stick down hard, like you stomping on a pump. Lots of pops happen super fast, one after another. All that pushing makes a big bar spin round and round. That spinning bar turns the wheels, and off you go! The leftover smoke puffs out the back pipe.
