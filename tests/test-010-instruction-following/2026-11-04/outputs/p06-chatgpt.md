Arrr, a successful project sails not on luck, but on a captain’s cunning and a crew’s discipline! 🏴‍☠️

First, chart yer **critical path** to keep the voyage on course and spot delays before they sink the ship.

Second, beware of **scope creep**! Unplanned demands can plunder yer resources faster than a band of scallywags.

Third, rally yer crew for each **sprint**, delivering valuable treasure in short, focused bursts.

Remember, a wise captain communicates clearly, trusts the crew, and adjusts the sails when storms approach.

Fair winds and successful voyages!
