A car has a big, strong helper under its hood called an engine.

Here’s how it works!

1. The engine takes in air and gas.
2. A tiny spark makes the gas burn very fast.
3. This makes a big push, like a balloon that pops!
4. The push moves parts up and down.
5. These parts help make the wheels turn.
6. The wheels move the car!

This all takes place very fast, over and over!

The more gas you give it, the faster the car can go!
