🔥 **BATCH SIMILAR TASKS** — Group emails, calls, and admin work into dedicated time blocks to reduce context switching.

🐸 **EAT THE FROG FIRST** — Tackle your hardest or most dreaded task at the start of the day while your energy and focus are highest.

⏱️ **WORK IN FOCUSED SPRINTS** — Use 25-to-50-minute blocks of uninterrupted work followed by short breaks to sustain concentration without burning out.

📵 **SILENCE YOUR NOTIFICATIONS** — Turn off nonessential alerts during deep work so your attention stays on the task instead of your phone.

📝 **PLAN TOMORROW TONIGHT** — Spend five minutes at the end of each day listing your top priorities so you can start the next morning with clear direction.
