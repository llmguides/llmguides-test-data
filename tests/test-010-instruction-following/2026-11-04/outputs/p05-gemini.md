**Paris** rewards those who step off the postcard trail and into the rhythm of its distinct neighborhoods. Lose an afternoon inside the glass-roofed *passages couverts*, historic 19th-century arcades packed with rare bookshops, stamp collectors, and bustling bistros. Climb the cobblestone slopes of Belleville for sweeping city views and vibrant street art, or claim a green metal chair beneath the chestnut trees of the Jardin du Luxembourg. From subterranean jazz clubs in the Latin Quarter to the sun-drenched terraces of Canal Saint-Martin, where locals gather over natural wine and farmhouse cheese, the French capital thrives on everyday discovery and effortless charm.
