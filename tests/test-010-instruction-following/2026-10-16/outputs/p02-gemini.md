A car engine works like your legs on a bike, but it uses tiny fire pops instead of your feet!

Inside the car's big metal box, there are strong cups that hold metal blocks. First, a splash of car juice and a breath of fresh air go into a cup. Next, a tiny spark makes that mix go **POP!**

That hot pop pushes the metal block down really hard and fast. The block pushes a long bar, and that bar spins the car's wheels. Thousands of tiny pops happen every minute to make the car zoom down the road!
