Atmospheric carbon dioxide concentrations have increased by over 50% since preindustrial times.

Burning fossil fuels releases carbon dioxide, a major greenhouse gas.

Climate change has raised global average temperatures by approximately 1.3°C since preindustrial times.

Deforestation contributes to climate change by reducing Earth's carbon storage capacity.

Earth's global mean sea level rose approximately 20 centimeters during 1901–2018.
