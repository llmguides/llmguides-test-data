A car has a big box under its hood called an engine.

Here’s how it works!

1. The engine takes in air and gas.
2. A tiny spark makes the gas and air go BOOM!
3. That boom pushes a part down.
4. This makes other parts spin.
5. The spin helps turn the car’s wheels.
6. The wheels move, and off you go!

This all takes place very fast, many times each second!

It's like a bunch of tiny booms that work as a team to make your car go VROOM!
