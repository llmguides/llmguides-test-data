Reading offers remarkable benefits for both mind and spirit. It expands vocabulary, sharpens focus, and strengthens memory as readers follow complex ideas and narratives. Books transport us to unfamiliar places, building empathy by letting us experience lives unlike our own. Regular reading also reduces stress, providing a calming escape from daily pressures. Furthermore, it fuels curiosity and lifelong learning, keeping our thinking flexible and sharp. Ultimately, a good book truly enriches every stage of life.
