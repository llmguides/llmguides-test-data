⏱️ **USE THE POMODORO TECHNIQUE** — Work in focused twenty-five-minute intervals separated by five-minute breaks to prevent mental fatigue.

🐸 **EAT THE FROG FIRST** — Tackle your most challenging and important task first thing in the morning before distractions arise.

📵 **ELIMINATE DIGITAL DISTRACTIONS** — Put your phone in another room and use website blockers during deep work sessions to maintain unbroken focus.

⚡ **APPLY THE TWO-MINUTE RULE** — Complete any task immediately if it takes less than two minutes rather than adding it to your to-do list.

🌙 **PLAN TOMORROW TONIGHT** — Write down your top three priorities at the end of each workday so you can start the next morning with immediate momentum.
