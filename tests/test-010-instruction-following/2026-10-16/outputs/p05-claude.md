Paris rewards the curious. Wander the covered passages of the 19th century, glass-roofed arcades hiding antique toy shops and secondhand booksellers. Descend into the Catacombs, where millions of bones line silent tunnels beneath the streets. Climb Belleville at dusk for street art and views across rooftops, then join locals for natural wine in a tiny bar. Browse the Saint-Ouen flea market, the world's largest, for vintage treasures. Picnic in Parc des Buttes-Chaumont among cliffs and a temple-topped island. Catch jazz in a vaulted Latin Quarter cellar. Stay a week and every arrondissement starts to feel like its own private village.
