A car needs food, just like you. Its food is gas. Inside the car is a big metal box. Gas goes in and gets a tiny spark, which makes a small, fast boom. Each boom pushes a metal stick down, hard. Lots of booms happen, one after the other, super fast, like popcorn popping. All that pushing makes a rod spin round and round. The spinning rod is hooked to the wheels, so the wheels turn too. And off you go! More gas means more booms, and the faster you zoom.
