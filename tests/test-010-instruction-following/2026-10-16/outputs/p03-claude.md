🔥 **BATCH SIMILAR TASKS** — Group emails, calls, and admin work into dedicated time blocks to reduce context switching.

🐸 **EAT THE FROG FIRST** — Tackle your hardest or most important task at the start of the day while your energy and focus are highest.

⏱️ **USE THE TWO-MINUTE RULE** — If a task takes less than two minutes, do it immediately instead of adding it to your to-do list.

📵 **SILENCE NOTIFICATIONS DURING DEEP WORK** — Turn off alerts on your phone and computer so you can sustain uninterrupted concentration on complex work.

📝 **PLAN TOMORROW TONIGHT** — Spend five minutes at the end of each day listing your top priorities so you can start the next morning with clear direction.
