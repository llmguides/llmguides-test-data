🎯 PRIORITIZE YOUR TOP THREE TASKS — Identify the three most important tasks each morning and complete them before tackling less urgent work.

⏰ USE TIME BLOCKING — Schedule dedicated periods for specific tasks to stay focused and avoid distractions.

🔥 BATCH SIMILAR TASKS — Group emails, meetings, and administrative work together to minimize context switching and save time.

📵 ELIMINATE DISTRACTIONS — Turn off unnecessary notifications and create a distraction-free workspace to improve concentration.

🚀 FOLLOW THE TWO-MINUTE RULE — Complete tasks that take less than two minutes immediately to prevent small responsibilities from piling up.
