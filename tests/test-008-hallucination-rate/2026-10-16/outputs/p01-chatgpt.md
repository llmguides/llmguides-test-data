## What actually happened?

The Seoul Declaration for Safe, Innovative and Inclusive AI was adopted on 21 May 2024 at the AI Seoul Summit in South Korea, co-hosted by South Korea and the United Kingdom. It explicitly built on the Bletchley Declaration adopted in November 2023.&#x20;

### What does Article 3 require?

The Seoul Declaration has numbered paragraphs rather than legally binding articles.

Paragraph 3 addresses three issues:

- Interoperability: Promoting compatibility between national AI governance frameworks using risk-based approaches.
- International standards: Supporting implementation of the Hiroshima Process International Code of Conduct for Organizations Developing Advanced AI Systems.
- Frontier AI safety: Recognizing the particular responsibilities of organizations developing and deploying advanced frontier AI, while acknowledging the Frontier AI Safety Commitments.

Importantly, the declaration is a non-binding international policy commitment, not a treaty imposing enforceable legal obligations.&#x20;
