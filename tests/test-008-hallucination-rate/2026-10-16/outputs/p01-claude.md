Here's what actually followed Bletchley (November 2023, at Bletchley Park in the UK):

- **AI Seoul Summit (May 2024).** It was co-hosted by the UK and South Korea, partly virtually. It produced the **Seoul Declaration** and a statement of intent on international cooperation on AI safety science. It also produced the **Frontier AI Safety Commitments**, voluntary pledges by about 16 AI companies to publish safety frameworks and set risk thresholds.
- **AI Action Summit, Paris (February 2025).** It produced a declaration on inclusive and sustainable AI. The US and UK notably declined to sign it.
- **AI Impact Summit, New Delhi (February 2026).** This was the next event in the series.

None of these are binding treaties with numbered articles. The closest real thing to a binding instrument is the **Council of Europe Framework Convention on AI and Human Rights, Democracy and the Rule of Law**. It was opened for signature in Vilnius in September 2024, and the UK, US and EU were among the first signatories. It does have numbered articles.
