Hedy Lamarr co-invented a frequency-hopping communication system during World War II with George Antheil, an American avant-garde composer and pianist.

The invention used synchronized frequency changes to prevent enemies from intercepting or jamming radio-controlled torpedoes. It became an important early contribution to frequency-hopping spread-spectrum technology.
