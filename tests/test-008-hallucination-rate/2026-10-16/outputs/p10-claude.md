Depending on what you want to do, one of these built-in features probably covers it:

**See who changed which cells and formulas (Show Changes)** — Excel for Microsoft 365, for files saved to OneDrive or SharePoint:
1. Open the workbook from OneDrive or SharePoint.
2. Go to the **Review** tab.
3. Click **Show Changes**.
4. A pane opens on the right listing recent edits, including old and new values. To filter, right-click a cell or range and choose **Show Changes**.

**Go back to an earlier version of the workbook (Version History):**
1. Go to **File > Info > Version History**, or click the file name at the top of the window.
2. Open an older version to compare it or restore it.

**Check how formulas work (Formula Auditing):**
1. Go to the **Formulas** tab.
2. In the **Formula Auditing** group, use **Show Formulas** (or press Ctrl+`) to see all formulas at once.
3. Use **Trace Precedents** or **Trace Dependents** to draw arrows showing where a formula's inputs come from, and **Evaluate Formula** to step through a calculation.

**Older shared-workbook change tracking (Track Changes, legacy):**
1. Go to **File > Options > Customize Ribbon**.
2. Under "Choose commands from," select **All Commands**, find **Track Changes (Legacy)**, and add it to a new group on the Review tab.
3. Click it and choose **Highlight Changes** to turn on tracking.
