Here are three hooks, each built on a different approach:

**Hook 1: Surprising data point**
Nearly 9 in 10 HR teams using AI in recruiting say it makes them faster. Only about 1 in 4 job seekers trust it to judge them fairly, and that gap is where hiring is going wrong.

**Hook 2: Contrarian take**
Everyone's worried AI will screen out great candidates. I'm more worried it's teaching great candidates to write for robots instead of for people.

**Hook 3: Personal story or observation**
Last month, a candidate asked me if a human would ever actually read her application. I paused a second too long before answering, and that pause told me how much hiring has changed.

Sources for Hook 1: 89% of HR pros using AI in recruiting say it saves time or boosts efficiency (SHRM), and only 26 percent of applicants trust AI to evaluate them fairly in 2026. These come from different surveys, so keep that in mind if anyone pushes back on the comparison. Hook 3 is a template, so change the details to match something that really happened to you.
