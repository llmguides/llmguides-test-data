**Executive Summary: Q4 2025**

Q4 demonstrated strong top-line momentum, generating **$4.2M in revenue** (up 12% QoQ), anchored by a landmark **$800K ARR enterprise deal with Acme Corp**.

However, margin compression and retention pressures require immediate strategic focus. **Operating costs rose 18% to $3.1M**, outpacing revenue growth as headcount expanded to 85. Customer acquisition softened to **340 new logos** (-5%), while **churn increased to 4.2%** (from 3.1%) alongside an **NPS decline to 72**. Additionally, the departure of two senior engineers introduces near-term technical execution risk.

Leadership is prioritizing cost stabilization, engineering continuity, and retention initiatives to safeguard operating leverage entering Q1.
